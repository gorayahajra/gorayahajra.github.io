About

Accuracy that institutions rely on

I verify financial data for a living. My team’s clients, among them hedge funds, asset managers and investment banks, trade on what we publish, so every insider filing, block trade and corporate action has to be right.

I also watch the automated bots that capture this data, catching scraping errors, misaligned fields and failed ingestion before they reach a client. That mix of high-volume checking and rule-based governance is what I now want to apply to AI safety research: governance frameworks, third-party model examination and technical disclosure standards.
